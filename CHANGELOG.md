# Changelog

All notable changes are listed here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions
follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-22

Bug fixes and optimizations.

- Your setup is safer: the first tab after a browser restart shows your own
  page at once, saves from several tabs no longer undo each other, and a
  file that isn't a Tarn backup can't replace your setup.
- The search box's Enter always does what it says, including with
  Japanese, Chinese and Korean input.
- "Use my location" in Weather works in Chrome.
- When the animation can't run, or the computer can't keep up, you see a
  real picture of the wallpaper instead of a smear of its colors.
- Full keyboard and screen reader support in settings, the search box and
  the guide, with AA contrast in light mode.
- Lighter: hidden tabs do no work at all, visible tabs do about half the
  idle work, and the background script is 30x smaller.
- Tarn may reach only its approved services (weather and Google Lens).
- New installs open on Silk in light mode and on Tarn in dark mode,
  switching with your system until you pick a wallpaper.
- With settings open, the search box has the same × as the widgets.
- Moving or resizing a widget no longer sticks to the pointer, and the
  first-run guide shows the real way to hide Chrome's footer.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-20

The first public release.

- Sixteen living WebGL2 wallpapers, or your own photos, each with a gauge
  for how much it asks of your computer.
- Fifteen clock faces, or no clock.
- Widgets on a calm grid: bookmarks (your bookmarks bar from the first
  tab), notes, tasks, calendar, timer, weather and custom shader widgets.
- A search box like your browser's: your search engine, your bookmarks as
  you type, voice, Google Lens and AI Mode.
- Settings in the browser's side panel, with search, light and dark
  appearance, keyboard shortcuts, automatic backups, export and import.
- Ten languages.
- A short first-run guide.
- No accounts and no tracking. Tarn goes online only for what you ask:
  searches, voice, Lens and AI Mode when you use them, and Weather for the
  place you pick.
