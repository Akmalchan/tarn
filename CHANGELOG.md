# Changelog

All notable changes are listed here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions
follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-20

The first public release.

- Sixteen living WebGL2 wallpapers, or your own photos, each with a gauge
  for how much it asks of your computer.
- Fifteen clock faces, or no clock.
- Widgets on a calm grid: bookmarks (your bookmarks bar from the first
  tab), notes, tasks, calendar, timer, weather and custom shader widgets.
- A search box like your browser's: your search engine, your bookmarks as
  you type, voice, Google Lens and AI Mode.
- Settings in the browser's side panel, with search, light and dark
  appearance, keyboard shortcuts, automatic backups, export and import.
- Ten languages.
- A short first-run guide.
- No accounts and no tracking. Weather is the only feature that goes
  online, and only once you add it.
